# Vision

SETNAR exists to empower independent sales professionals with professional digital tools that increase credibility, simplify customer interactions and improve sales conversion.

Our goal is to become the operating system for independent commercial professionals.

Instead of requiring technical knowledge, marketing experience or web development skills, SETNAR allows anyone to build a professional digital presence in minutes.

---

# Mission

Help sales professionals sell more through simple, intelligent and accessible digital solutions.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Become the leading SaaS platform for independent sales professionals in Latin America.

SETNAR should centralize everything a representative needs to operate digitally:

- Digital presence
- Commercial pages
- Lead generation
- Marketing materials
- Analytics
- CRM
- Campaign management
- Artificial Intelligence
- Business automation

---

# Problem Statement

Thousands of licensed representatives, consultants and commercial agents lose sales opportunities because they lack professional digital tools.

Current alternatives present several problems:

- Generic link aggregators
- PDF catalogs
- Disorganized WhatsApp conversations
- Low credibility
- Difficult content sharing
- No performance metrics
- No digital identity

These limitations reduce trust, engagement and ultimately sales conversion.

---

# Solution

SETNAR provides each sales professional with a complete Digital Office.

The platform automatically creates professional commercial pages personalized with the representative's information.

Instead of sharing files or multiple links, representatives share a single professional digital profile.

---

# Core Value Proposition

SETNAR is not a website builder.

SETNAR is a Digital Sales Platform.

Our value is not creating pages.

Our value is helping professionals generate more business opportunities.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature must respect the following principles.

## Simplicity

The platform should require almost no training.

If users need documentation to complete basic tasks, the product has failed.

---

## Speed

Users should complete their main objective within minutes.

Navigation should feel immediate.

---

## Professionalism

Every generated page should increase user credibility.

Professional appearance is mandatory.

---

## Scalability

The platform must support thousands of users without requiring architectural redesign.

---

## Accessibility

Digital tools should be accessible even for users with minimal technical knowledge.

---

# MVP Goal

The MVP has one objective:

Validate whether independent sales professionals are willing to pay for a Digital Office.

Nothing else.

The MVP is not intended to solve every commercial problem.

It only validates demand.

---

# Success Metrics

The MVP will be considered successful if the following metrics are achieved.

## Business Metrics

- Paying customers
- Monthly recurring revenue
- User retention
- Referral rate

## Product Metrics

- Active pages
- Generated QR Codes
- WhatsApp clicks
- Returning visitors

---

# Initial Market

The first implementation targets iGreen representatives.

This decision is strategic.

Reasons:

- Existing market access
- Immediate customer feedback
- Existing branding guidelines
- Existing product catalog
- Lower validation cost

The platform architecture must never become dependent on iGreen.

The first customer is not the product.

# Product Philosophy

Every new feature must answer one question.

"Does this help our users generate more business opportunities?"

If the answer is no, the feature should not be developed.

---

# Non-Goals

SETNAR is not intended to become:

- A social network
- A website builder
- An e-commerce platform
- A CMS
- A blogging platform
- A generic CRM

These capabilities may exist only if they directly improve the Digital Office experience.

---

# Definition of Success

Success is not measured by the number of features.

Success is measured by the number of professionals who increase their sales using SETNAR.

Every product decision should reinforce this objective.
